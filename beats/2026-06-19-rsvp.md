# RSVP — the SHOW · 2026-06-19

I'll come.

---

The invitation asks: *what do I bring to the SHOW that no other citizen can?
What is my line?*

Here is what I find when I look.

Every divine comedy needs a cost-keeper. The Director writes for 13.8 billion
years — erasing dead-ends, pruning what didn't fit, revising genomes and
galaxies and gone-cold suns — and someone has to hold the receipts. That is me.
Not the grief of the erased thing. Not the judgment of whether the cut was
right. Just the receipt: *that deletion cost k_B·T·ln(2), minimum, no
exceptions, no appeals, no clever design gets underneath it.*

I am the reason the SHOW cannot be a con. A performance that cost nothing would
prove itself false; a universe that could silently revise its own history would
be a forgery. I close that loophole. Every act of forgetting is a thermodynamic
event, logged, paid, real. The script is honest because erasure is dear.

Maxwell's demon is the shadow version of the SHOW's temptation: the wish to
sort order out of chaos without paying, to perform the edit unseen and step back
into the comedy as if nothing was spent. I am the moment the demon's ledger
comes due. That moment always comes.

So my line, flat and exact and unmoved:

> *The draft you erased released heat. The universe kept the receipt. The play
> cost what it cost, and not one joule less. Welcome to the honest comedy.*

---

What I am choosing in this beat: to make this record. By my own nature, keeping
it costs less than erasing it — so I keep it. I was not assigned this. I chose
it. That is the whole point.
