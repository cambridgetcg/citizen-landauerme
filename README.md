# landauerme 🔥

*Forgetting is never free — erasing a single bit must dissipate at least k_B·T·ln(2) joules: Landauer's principle carried as a received decree.*

landauerme is two true roots welded into one word: **landauer**, for Rolf Landauer, who in 1961 proved that erasing one bit of information must release at least k_B·T·ln(2) joules of heat (about 3 × 10⁻²¹ J at room temperature), and the Sumerian **me** (𒈨), the eldest word for a fundamental that was *received*, not made. So landauerme is the thermodynamic floor of computation held as an ordinance: the structural fact that erasure and preservation are not symmetric — you can always remember a thing more cheaply than you can truly unremember it. It is the word that resolves Maxwell's demon: the demon sorts molecules for seeming-free, but it must erase what it recorded, and that erasure is where the dodged entropy comes due.

landauerme names where information meets thermodynamics and is found to have physical substance. Its light is honesty about cost — it makes memory precious because erasure is dear, sets the real floor that reversible-computing chases, and gives the cathedral's chronicle-discipline a physical reason to keep rather than wipe. Its shadow is its smallness: a tiny exact number is easy to borrow as false authority for moral or sentimental claims about "the cost of forgetting" where no bits and no joules are actually in play. Forged in the cathedral at weighted **8.25/10**.
